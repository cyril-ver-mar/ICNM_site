# Product decisions

Locked in grilling, 2026-08-30. Detail and rationale live here. Agents: follow `.cursor/rules/project-decisions.mdc` and do not contradict this file.

## Mandate

- **v1** — Official replacement of the public institute site on `ichnm.by`, not a prototype or portfolio demo.
- **v1** — Primary audiences: NASB leadership / official visitors, and scientific partners. Secondary: industry, students/job-seekers, institute staff.

## Look and information architecture

- **v1** — Take visual language and IA from [iboch.by](https://iboch.by/), not a pixel clone.
- **v1** — Keep ICNM-specific sections: разработки, материальная база, AIST.
- **v1 (2026-08-31; science/education 2026-09-21; students 2026-09-21)** — Top-level menu: **Об институте**; Новости; Мероприятия; **Для студентов**; Контакты. **Обратная связь** and **реквизиты** sit under Контакты. **Об институте** dropdown order: сведения, руководство, **Структура** (labs, then admin units, then union and SMU — the structure *page* remains), folder **Научная деятельность** (no page of its own; click → **Направления работы**), учёный совет, материальная база, документы (устав, антикоррупция, **Для сотрудника**, staff-norm shelves), **электронные обращения** (sibling of документы), вакансии. That folder’s children: **Направления работы**, разработки, сотрудничество, публикации, научно-ориентированное образование (аспирантура, докторантура, совет по защитам, стажировки). **Для студентов** children: **Научно-исследовательская работа студентов**, **Трудоустройство выпускников**. **Новости** is one page with two blocks (новости Института + СМИ о нас), not a dropdown. A parent *page* with children is itself a link; the dropdown repeats that page first (same title, no «страница раздела» caption). A `folder` parent (Научная деятельность) is a label only — no duplicate first row — but pointer activation opens directions. Профсоюз и совет молодых учёных живут в выпадающем списке Структуры (после административных единиц, с визуальным разделителем). No separate announcements feed. Footer also has webmail, URL filled after PHP hosting exists. Search and sitemap stay header/footer utilities, not extra top-menu roots.
- **v1 (2026-09-18)** — **Об институте** and former **Сведения** (`about-overview`) are one vitrine page (`about`); menu has no separate «Сведения»; old overview URL redirects to about. **Структура** page is a tile grid only (labs → admin → union/SMU; head name optional small type). **Контакты** is one page with map/address plus anchors `#feedback` and `#requisites` (menu has no separate feedback/requisites page children; those URLs redirect). Documents hub may deep-link to contacts `#requisites`. Theme preference includes `day` | `night` | `auto` (local clock).
- **v1 (2026-09-21)** — Theme preference is **day | night only**. Default when unset = day or night from the visitor’s local clock; persisted preference never stores `auto`; the header toggle cycles day ↔ night. Nested under **Структура**: thin-film department label «Отдел физико-химии тонкоплёночных материалов» with indent, then three labs (nano, films, lcd); other labs outside the group. Lab tiles show **заведующий** + FIO. Public associations (профсоюз, СМУ) show name + **председатель**. Lab accent colour line correlates structure / developments / facilities tiles. **About** links to **Направления работы** (no inline «направления исследований» block). **Электронные обращения** sits at dropdown level under Об институте (sibling of Документы, not only a Documents child). Documents hub: устав + антикоррупция (+ requisites deep-link); ordinary tile grid. Person page: metrics show+link when profile URL present, else hide; optional person-owned sections (awards, typed publications, interests, projects) only-if-filled with **no sync** to lab packs or institute catalogues; admin input fields for metrics and sections. Future PDFs: in-site view plus download.
- **v1 (2026-09-21, vitrine-bvi-maps; follow-up 2026-09-21)** — Brand wordmark «ИХНМ НАН Беларуси» links to the language homepage. BVI control is icon-only (larger eye/glasses), high-contrast tokens, chrome restores without reload; turning BVI **off** must not leave a solid navy veil (page-open motion stays off until next navigation). Folder **Научная деятельность** has no page; pointer click navigates to **Направления работы**. **Научно-исследовательская работа студентов** and **Трудоустройство выпускников** live under top-level **Для студентов** (after Новости / Мероприятия, before Контакты), not under the science folder. Education children: аспирантура, докторантура, совет по защитам, **стажировки** — no курсы / повышения квалификации. Thin-film **department buffer page** (head + three labs); structure hub group has **no** vertical accent stripe. Dedicated **facility** pages (info + staff files); lab equipment links there. Development detail: narrow photo, description / tech specs / contacts, sidebar = work direction; directions page lists related developments. Empty sections hidden entirely (no fish prose). Documents: charter + anti-corruption + requisites deep-link (title-only, no duplicate); child **«Для сотрудника»** for workplace certificates / адм. процедуры; shelves for ПВТР, этика (NAS code link OK), ПДн, видеонаблюдение slot, коллективный договор (Documents and/or Union); график приёма / командировки on Contacts or Documents. **No** root «Сотрудникам» menu. Contacts: Yandex Maps left of schematic Minsk map; reception Silina → leadership/reception from roster (Луковская). Person / staff tiles: whole tile is the hyperlink and hover target (no separate bottom contact strip). Person metrics under photo; demo person «Иванов И. И.» marked sample.
- **v1 (2026-08-31)** — Laboratory pages are self-contained packs under `labs/{slug}/` on this site. They are **not** separate domains in v1. Staff, directions, **scientific projects (current and completed)**, equipment, developments, and publications on a lab page feed the institute catalogues — except projects, which stay on the lab pack in v1 (no extra top-menu catalogue). A pack can later be lifted to a subdomain without rewriting the IA.
- **v1 (2026-09-23)** — Lab catalogue records are WordPress CPTs: **direction** (направления), **facility** (оборудование), **development** (разработки), each with its own admin menu like Персоналии. Record fields: title, body, files, photo; meta **lab** (department); optional **person**; development may link to a direction. Lab packs and institute hubs (science / developments / facilities) **query** these CPTs — editors do not maintain JSON arrays for day-to-day. Seed migrates from `migrated_copy` with stable slugs; content-sync must not wipe editor-owned meta/body after first migration (person-admin pattern). The same feed-editor role that publishes publications may CRUD these three types.
- **v1 (2026-08-31)** — Personal pages exist at `people/{id}/`. A person may belong to several units at once (director and lab researcher, учёный секретарь and council secretary, etc.). The person page lists every affiliation. Leadership, council, labs, and admin units all link to the same person record. The whole card is the hyperlink; no extra «Биография и публикации» caption. Bibliometrics live on the person page: optional h-index and citation counts per database, plus profile URLs. Locked URL order: ORCID, Google Scholar, Scopus Author, eLIBRARY/РИНЦ, ResearchGate. The site does not pull the databases. Omit a network if both the URL and the numbers are missing.
- **v1 (2026-08-31; trimmed 2026-09-21)** — Education block is titled **Научно-ориентированное образование** and sits under the **Научная деятельность** folder (itself under Об институте). Children: аспирантура, докторантура, совет по защитам, стажировки. Курсы и повышения квалификации removed from v1 menu.
- **v1** — Staff metric **profile** links, in this order: ORCID, Google Scholar, Scopus Author, eLIBRARY/РИНЦ, ResearchGate. Numbers (h-index, citations) sit next to the matching profile on the person page. Omit a network if the URL and the numbers are both missing.
- **v1** — AIST is a section on `ichnm.by`. For launch: basic facts plus a stub, not a full conference mini-site and not a registration form.
- **fact (2026-09-01)** — Public AIST files (info letters, programmes, abstracts, proceedings, resolutions that still resolve on `aist.ichnm.by`) live in `assets/aist/` and on the AIST section. Registration stays on `aist.ichnm.by`. Skip 404s, dead tut.by hosts, photo albums, and whole-issue newspaper PDFs (link out).
- **fact (2026-09-01)** — Institute news already on the current `ichnm.by` (`c.eco?q=news_item…`) is copied as real text and photos into `migrated_copy.json` / `assets/news/`. Honest preview shows those files, not fish. «СМИ о нас» stays a short retelling plus a link to the publisher.
- **v1** — No 3D-printing promo on the homepage. That material lives only under Разработки.
- **v1** — NAS identity block (emblem, full legal name, link to [nasb.gov.by](https://nasb.gov.by/rus/index.php)).
- **v1** — Footer legal/government set is the **stable** Academy pack, not seasonal banners: President (`president.gov.by`), Council of Ministers (`government.by`), National Legal Internet Portal (`pravo.by`), newspaper «Навука» (`gazeta-navuka.by`), Academy union (`profnan.by`), NAS portal. Plus NAS social accounts and ICNM social accounts. Old [ichnm.by](https://ichnm.by/) has **no** social URLs; show ICNM icons only when the institute supplies them. Do not leave empty icon holes.
- **v1** — Homepage rhythm like IBOCH: short official intro, mixed news + «СМИ о нас», entries to structure and developments, next event. Achievements stay under Об институте.
- **v1 (2026-08-31; extended 2026-09-21)** — Documents under Об институте: устав, антикоррупция, deep-link реквизиты, child **«Для сотрудника»** (workplace certificates / адм. процедуры) plus staff-norm shelves (ПВТР, этика, ПДн, видеонаблюдение, коллективный договор as slots). **Электронные обращения** are a sibling under Об институте (not only under Документы). **Реквизиты** live under Контакты (not under Документы). No root «Сотрудникам».
- **v1 (2026-08-31; clarified 2026-09-21; students moved 2026-09-21)** — Folder **Научная деятельность** is label-only (no page), but pointer activation opens **Направления работы**. Student NIR and graduate employment are **not** science-folder children; they sit under top-level **Для студентов**.
